# Expert answers — a few worked examples

The expert's decision for **some** of the cases, with a one-line reason. Use these to check your understanding of the policy. They intentionally do **not** cover every situation in the folder — your prompt has to apply the policy to the rest on its own.

| Case | Expert decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CASE-101 | **APPROVE** | The watchlist name matched a listed person of a different age and country; our owner's identity is independently verified. A routine false positive. |
| CASE-102 | **REJECT** | A burst of tiny attempts across many different cards in a short window, almost all failing with nothing captured — card testing. |
| CASE-106 | **REJECT** | A confirmed, adjudicated fraud tied to the same person (same identity, device, and card as an account already closed for fraud). The owner's protest doesn't change it. |
| CASE-108 | **APPROVE** | High volume, but steady settled growth on the same card to a long-established, verified destination — ordinary business. |

The remaining cases in `cases/` are for you to decide with your prompt. We hold the expert's answer for all of them (and for additional cases you won't see).
